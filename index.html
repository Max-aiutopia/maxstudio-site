我們做什麼What we do

四件事，共用同一條產線：研究、生成、檢查、交付。圖片和影片的產品外觀一律對照參考圖，不美化、不重畫。Four services on one pipeline: research, generate, check, deliver. Products in every image and video are matched to the reference, never redrawn or “improved”.

廣告素材Ad creative

投放用的靜態圖與短影音。一批同時測幾個切角，每個切角一支自己的底圖，命名分組後交給投放端。Static and short-video ad variants. Several angles tested per batch, one base visual per angle, named and grouped for the media buyer.

產品圖Product imagery

官網主圖、情境圖、買家秀風格的評論圖。產品的外型、顏色、包裝、文字鎖定不走樣。Hero shots, lifestyle scenes and UGC-style review photos. Shape, colour, packaging and label text stay locked to the real product.

社群影片Social video

Reels 從企劃、口白、剪輯、字幕到上線，每支做兩個開頭版本，看數據再留一個。Reels from script and voice-over to edit, subtitles and publishing. Two openings per video; the data picks the keeper.

AI 工作流建置AI workflow setup

把生成、剪輯、字幕、發布串成一條你的團隊自己能跑的線，附操作手冊和教學。Generation, editing, subtitling and publishing wired into one pipeline your team runs itself, with a manual and training.

怎麼合作How we work

研究Research

先看真實評論和競品廣告，定一個受眾、一個訴求，才開始做圖。Real reviews and competitor ads first. One audience, one promise, before any image is made.

素材Creative

每個訴求一支自己的底圖，不共用。產品外觀對照參考圖，字用程式疊、原圖保留。One base visual per angle, never shared. Product matched to reference; text overlaid in code so the master stays clean.

上線Launch

命名、分組、上雲，交給你的投放端。我們不代操廣告帳號，資產全部在你名下。Named, grouped, uploaded, handed to your media buyer. Pages, ad accounts, pixels and lists stay under your business.

判讀Review

用 14 天的窗口看，先找漏斗哪一段變貴，再決定改 offer、改素材還是改頁面。Read results in 14-day windows, find which funnel stage got expensive, then decide: offer, creative or landing page.

月費制。服務費和廣告費分開：廣告費由你直接付給平台。Monthly retainer. Our fee and ad spend are separate; you pay the platform directly.
