Process怎麼合作

01

研究Research

先看真實評論和競品廣告，定一個受眾、一個訴求，才開始做圖。Real reviews and competitor ads first. One audience, one promise, before any image.

02

素材Creative

每個訴求一支自己的底圖，不共用。產品對照參考圖，字用程式疊，原圖保留。One base visual per angle, never shared. Product matched to reference; text overlaid in code.

03

上線Launch

命名、分組、上雲，交給你的投放端。資產全部在你名下。Named, grouped, uploaded, handed to your media buyer. Assets stay under your business.

04

判讀Review

用 14 天窗口看，先找漏斗哪一段變貴，再決定改 offer、改素材還是改頁面。14-day windows; find the funnel stage that got expensive, then decide: offer, creative or page.

月費制。服務費和廣告費分開，廣告費由你直接付給平台；粉專、廣告帳號、像素、名單開在你的商業檔案底下。Monthly retainer. Our fee and ad spend are separate; you pay the platform directly. Pages, ad accounts, pixels and lists live under your business.
