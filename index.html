Services服務內容

五件事，一個團隊做完。從研究同業開始，到每月把數據攤在桌上。

Five jobs, one team. From studying your peers to putting the numbers on the table every month.

01

同業研究Peer research

把同領域律師帳號的真實播放數抓下來，看什麼題目有人看、什麼形式留得住人、客人是怎麼從影片走到諮詢的。We pull the real view counts of lawyer accounts in your field: which topics get watched, which formats hold attention, and how viewers travel from a video to a consultation.

02

定位與規劃Positioning and plan

誰出鏡、主打哪一群人、第一個系列做什麼、每月產出多少、起步時間表。寫成一份規劃，律師看完就知道三個月會長什麼樣。Who appears on camera, which audience comes first, the opening series, monthly output and a start-up timeline, written as one plan the lawyer can read in ten minutes.

03

內容製作Content production

腳本、到事務所錄影、剪輯、字幕、封面、排程發佈。短影音、圖文輪播、長片、線上課程影片，同一組人做。Scripts, filming at your office, editing, subtitles, covers and scheduling. Short video, carousels, long-form and course videos, all by the same crew.

  • 三支置頂片先做：他是誰、幫誰解決什麼、他的經歷Three pinned videos first: who you are, who you help, what you have done
  • 每月固定數量，同步發到 Facebook 與 YouTube ShortsA fixed monthly count, mirrored to Facebook and YouTube Shorts
04

導流與線上課程Lead routing and courses

主頁、LINE 官方帳號歡迎訊息、連結頁、線上課程社群的開設與日常維護。入口固定放在同一個位置，片子裡不硬推。Profile, LINE official account welcome flow, link page, and the set-up and upkeep of your online-course community. One fixed entry point; no hard sell inside the videos.

05

數據月報與檢討Monthly report and review

每月一份：觀看、追蹤、諮詢或社群新增。開一次會，決定下個月改什麼。One report a month: views, followers, consultations or new members. One meeting to decide what changes next month.

Case案例：遺產繼承專科事務所

57個律師帳號的真實數據lawyer accounts, real numbers

一間專辦遺產繼承的事務所（台中、台北），2026 年 10 月啟動。社群帳號從零開始，目標是把影片的觀眾帶進律師的線上課程與諮詢。A firm specialising in inheritance and estate matters (Taichung and Taipei), started October 2026. Social accounts from zero; the goal is to move viewers into the lawyer's online course and consultations.

十天內交出：57 個台灣與海外律師帳號、每個近 90 天 36 支影片的播放與互動數據；定位建議；第一個系列的題目；每月產出與起步時間表；合約範圍。Delivered within ten days: view and engagement data for 57 lawyer accounts in Taiwan and abroad (36 videos each, last 90 days); positioning; the opening series; monthly output and a start-up timeline; the service scope.

研究看到的三件事，直接變成規劃。

Three findings from the research, written straight into the plan.

  1. 律師本人出鏡，比用事務所名義有人看。The lawyer on camera beats the firm's name.

    正在發片、粉絲破萬的台灣帳號裡，本人出鏡的觀看表現大約是事務所名義的兩倍。所以規劃讓律師固定出鏡，同一個座位、同一句收尾。Among active Taiwanese accounts above 10k followers, a lawyer appearing in person draws roughly twice the views of a firm-branded account. So the plan puts the lawyer on camera, same seat, same sign-off.

  2. 修法倒數是最有量的題目。A countdown to a legal change is the biggest topic.

    特留分修法 2027 年 2 月 17 日施行，是台灣社群上最有量的遺產題。第一個系列就做它，每個月一支倒數。The forced-heirship amendment takes effect on 17 February 2027 and is the most-watched inheritance topic in Taiwan. The opening series is built on it, one countdown video a month.

  3. 轉換路徑只有一條，入口要固定。There is one conversion path; the entry point must not move.

    台灣律師帳號的客人都是從影片走到 LINE，再到付費諮詢。所以主頁、文案、說明欄的入口放同一個位置，每支影片都一樣。Clients of Taiwanese law accounts travel from video to LINE to a paid consultation. So the entry point sits in the same place in the profile, captions and descriptions, every single video.

內容怎麼守律師倫理規範How the content stays within the bar's ethics rules
  • 不寫勝訴率，不保證結果，不用「幫客戶拿回多少錢」當標題No win rates, no guaranteed outcomes, no "we recovered X for a client" headlines
  • 案例一律改寫、去識別，不使用真實當事人的資料Cases are rewritten and anonymised; no real client data
  • 審理中的案子不評論；時事只講法律，不講個案輸贏No comment on pending matters; current events discussed as law, not as who won
  • 每支腳本和成片都經律師確認才上線Every script and final cut is approved by the lawyer before it goes live
