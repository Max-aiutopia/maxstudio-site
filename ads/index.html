Services服務內容

五件事，一個團隊做完。從客人的抱怨開始，到每天把數據攤在桌上。

Five jobs, one team. From the customer's complaint to the numbers on the table every day.

01

研究與切角Research and angles

先讀台灣消費者的真實抱怨：Dcard、PTT、蝦皮評論。鎖定一群人、一件最痛的事，寫成一句前置承諾和幾個切角。沒有研究文件，不開工做素材。We start with real complaints from Taiwanese shoppers on Dcard, PTT and Shopee reviews, lock one audience and one pain, and write it into a promise and a set of angles. No research document, no creative.

02

素材製作Creative production

靜態圖與影片，照台灣生活感做：不棚拍、不純白背景、不型錄置中。產品外型、顏色、包裝和參考圖一致。一批要並行測試的切角，每支都有自己的底圖。Stills and video with everyday Taiwanese texture: no studio lighting, no white sweep, no catalogue centring. The product matches the reference exactly. Every angle in a test batch gets its own visual.

  • 文案和圖上的字分開做，改字不重做圖Copy and on-image text are separate layers; changing a line never means redoing the image
  • 需要真人 UGC 時開分鏡表給創作者When real UGC is needed, creators get a shot list, not a guess
03

廣告投放Paid media

Meta 廣告的帳號結構、受眾、預算與排程。品項多的帳號用單一活動加品項分組，讓預算在品項之間自己流動，用數據挑贏家，不先押注。Account structure, audiences, budgets and scheduling on Meta. Multi-product accounts run as one campaign with product groups, so budget flows to what sells and the data picks the winners.

04

官網與落地頁素材Site and landing page assets

廣告把人帶到站上，站上要接得住。官網產品圖、商品詳情頁、Email 行銷圖、評論圖，同一組人做，風格跟廣告一致。Ads bring people to the site; the site has to hold them. Product photography, product page assets, email graphics and review images, by the same crew, in the same look as the ads.

05

成效判讀與月報Reading the numbers

先跟客戶拿貢獻毛利率，算出每個品項自己的損益線，再判哪組達標。每天同步的成效檢視台，每週一次判讀，每月一份報告：哪一段掉了、為什麼、下一步改什麼。We take the client's contribution margin and compute a break-even line per product before judging anything. A dashboard that refreshes daily, a reading every week, a report every month: what dropped, why, what changes next.

Case案例一：台灣生鮮水果電商

4.0Meta 歸因 ROAS · 3 個月Meta attributed ROAS · 3 months

一家台灣生鮮水果電商，2026 年 6 月 19 日到 9 月 18 日。3 個月廣告花費 NT$444,001，Meta 歸因購買 989 筆、營業額約 NT$178 萬，整體 ROAS 4.0。另外交付 383 張官網產品圖，已接上官網。A Taiwanese fresh-fruit retailer, 19 June to 18 September 2026. NT$444,001 of ad spend in three months, 989 attributed purchases, about NT$1.78 million of attributed revenue, ROAS 4.0 overall. Plus 383 product photos delivered and now on the store.

口徑：Meta 歸因，點擊 7 天、瀏覽 1 天；營業額由花費乘 ROAS 回推。9 月到 18 日止。策略、素材、每日判讀由我們做，媒體採買由合作的投放端執行。Basis: Meta attribution, 7-day click and 1-day view; revenue derived from spend times ROAS. September runs to the 18th. Strategy, creative and daily readings were ours; media buying was executed by a partner buyer.

四件事，決定了這個帳號的數字。

Four decisions made these numbers.

  1. 帳號結構先重建。Rebuild the account first.

    接手時一個品項一個活動，訊號被切碎、每組都在學習期打轉。改成單一活動、品項分組，預算在品項之間自己流動，用廣鋪測試讓數據挑贏家。We inherited one campaign per product: fragmented signal, every ad set stuck in learning. We rebuilt it as one campaign with product groups so budget flows between products and a broad test lets the data choose.

  2. 一句前置承諾，打遍整批素材。One promise across the whole batch.

    從 Dcard、PTT、蝦皮評論找到最痛的一件事：開箱是一整箱壓傷的爛果。整批素材統一「壞一顆，賠一顆」。當時同業沒有人把這句放在檯面上。The single worst complaint on Dcard, PTT and Shopee: opening the box to bruised, rotten fruit. Every creative carried the same promise, one bad fruit, one replaced. Nobody in the category was saying it out loud.

  3. 換切角就贏，數據確認。Change the angle, win. The data agreed.

    水蜜桃第二代切角累計 ROAS 4.69，打贏原始組的 3.87；同批「不是所有水蜜桃」單支做到 6.63。同一個品項、同一個受眾池，只換了切角。The second-generation peach angle ran at 4.69 against the original group's 3.87; "not all peaches are the same" reached 6.63 on its own. Same product, same audience pool, only the angle changed.

  4. 沒成的也留著。The losers stay in the record.

    大西瓜組 ROAS 0.45，測完就關。紅毛丹 6 支影片被演算法停掉，7 天只花了 149 元。這些都在同一份資料裡，拿得出來。The watermelon group ran at 0.45 and was cut. Six rambutan videos were starved by the algorithm, NT$149 spent in seven days. All of it sits in the same dataset and can be shown.

Case案例二：企業主培訓機構

自然流量帶人進門，廣告帶合格的名單。

Organic brings people in; ads bring qualified leads.

2026 年 8 到 9 月，以內容規劃師身分進駐一間企業主培訓機構，負責社群內容與獲客廣告的規劃。對象是有實體店、有員工的老闆，產品是高單價的課程與陪跑，所以名單要先篩，不是越多越好。August to September 2026, embedded as content planner at a training provider for business owners. The audience is shop owners with staff; the product is high-ticket courses and coaching, so leads are qualified first, not maximised.

哪裡沒成，也講 · 成人保健電商，2026 年 5 到 7 月Where it did not work · adult wellness retailer, May to July 2026

素材很強，生意沒成立。Strong creative, no business.

主力素材點擊率 8.2% 到 10.4%、每次點擊 NT$3 到 4，以台灣電商來說是前段。但帶進 136 次加入購物車，只成交 33 筆。問題在商品頁、價格帶與結帳，不在鉤子、不在受眾。累計沒打平，雙方結束合作，整段資料完整封存。Lead creatives ran at 8.2 to 10.4 percent click rate and NT$3 to 4 per click, top tier for Taiwanese ecommerce. They brought 136 add-to-carts and 33 orders. The break was on the product page, the price point and checkout, not the hook or the audience. It never broke even; the engagement ended and the full record was archived.

哪裡沒成，也講 · 居家香氛品牌，2026 年 7 到 8 月Where it did not work · home fragrance brand, July to August 2026

第二週推翻了自己的第一版診斷。Week two overturned our own diagnosis.

第一週判「演算法把預算餵給了最弱的素材」。四天後預算確實移了，帳號 ROAS 反而從 0.80 掉到 0.71，兩支主力趨同。那是小樣本的假優勢。所以建議改成先停下來看定價、運費與落地頁，不再在素材層花錢。Week one said the algorithm was feeding the weakest creative. Four days later the budget had moved and account ROAS fell from 0.80 to 0.71, with the two leads converging. A small-sample illusion. The advice changed: stop, look at pricing, shipping and the landing page, and stop spending at the creative layer.

數字怎麼給How we report numbers
  • 只用廣告後台的原始數字，不加工、不估算；每個數字都能回到後台對Only raw numbers from the ad platform, no massaging; every figure can be checked against the dashboard
  • 講清楚是平台歸因還是店家實收，兩個不一樣We always say whether it is platform attribution or booked revenue; they differ
  • 客戶的毛利率、損益線與成本結構不對外，連匿名也不放Client margins, break-even lines and cost structure never leave the room, not even anonymised
  • 沒成的案子一樣留在紀錄裡，而且會說為什麼Failed accounts stay in the record, with the reason
